How to test

Tap geometry to select it, then Delete Selected to remove it. Pinch with two fingers to zoom, or slide two fingers together to pan. With no tool selected, drag with one finger to pan. Curve handles appear immediately.

1. Add a few points.
2. Choose Line, then tap two points.
3. Choose Curve and tap two points, then Select / Move to drag its blue handles.
4. Drag endpoints to reshape lines and curves.
4. Edit coordinates for precise placement.
5. Try undo, redo, zoom and pan.
