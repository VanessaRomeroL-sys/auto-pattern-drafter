Experimental elastic response, not physical cloth simulation

What you're seeing: a procedurally detailed avatar with modeled facial features and hair, plus a generated skirt mesh with real lighting, shading, and adjustable silhouette. This is a geometry/rendering prototype, not a photorealistic or scanned human avatar or a physically simulated garment. It does not yet calculate body measurements, collision, drape, fabric stretch, or sewing-pattern fit. The folds and finger-driven deformation are mathematically modeled with spring-like relaxation, not a validated physical cloth simulation. Seam guides are illustrative, not sewn pattern edges.
